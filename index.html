An exploration of the unseen, inspired by synesthesia and born from my fascination with hidden dimensions of reality. I am drawn to the invisible layers that exist beneath the surface of our perceived world, seeking to reveal the magic that lies within. Through my experiments, I modify and transform nature's sounds and light, discovering hidden colors and frequencies that exist within them. These light experiments blend visual and auditory experiences, traversing the elements - fire, earth, water, ether, and air. It is an interplay of light, color, and sound as they reveal unexpected connections between sensory realms, offering glimpses into the subtle dimensions that surround us.
